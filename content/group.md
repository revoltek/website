The work of my research group is focused on exploring the ultra-low frequency emission from astronomical objects.

- **Dr. Virginia Cuciti** (von Humboldt fellow): statistical studies of radio halos and emission from galaxy clusters at low-frequency.
- **Alexander Jones** (PhD): radio relics and diffuse radio sources.
- **Thomas Pasini** (PhD): radio and X-ray from galaxy groups.
- **Henrik Edler** (MSc): simulation of LOFAR 2.0 data, ultra-low frequency observations.
- **Kevin Laudamus** (BSc): machine learning techniques applied to radio imaging.
