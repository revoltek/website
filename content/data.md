## Data from published papers

LOFAR LBA Sky Survey (de Gasperin+ A&A, 648, A104)  
**Data:** <https://www.lofar-surveys.org/lba.html>

Diffuse Radio Emission from Galaxy Clusters (van Weeren, de Gasperin+ Space Science Reviews, 215, id.16)  
**Data:** <http://www.galaxyclusters.com>

A radio spectral index map and catalogue at 147–1400 MHz covering 80 per cent of the sky (de Gasperin+ MNRAS 474, 5008)  
**Data:** <http://tgssadr.strw.leidenuniv.nl/spidx>
